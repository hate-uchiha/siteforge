Questions

Frequently asked

How soon can you start?

Most bookings are booked within 24 to 48 hours. Urgent calls get same day priority whenever the schedule allows.

Do you give a fixed price before starting?

Yes. You get a written quote before any work begins. The price does not change unless you approve extra work in writing first.

Are you insured?

Fully insured and happy to send proof of cover on request. Every job is done by our own team, never subcontracted out without telling you.

Which areas do you cover?

Every area listed on this page, plus most places nearby. If you are just outside the list, ask anyway, we often travel a little further.

What payment methods do you take?

Card, bank transfer, and cash. No deposit is taken for standard jobs, and you get a proper invoice for every visit.

Need it sorted this week?

Tell us what you need and we will send a written price, usually the same day.
