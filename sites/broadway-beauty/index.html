What we do

Beauty salon done properly

Every job below is quoted in writing before we start. If it turns out to be something else, we tell you before touching anything.

Facials

Cleanse, exfoliate, extractions, and a mask chosen for your skin on the day rather than off a list.

Brow shaping and tinting

Wax, thread, or tweeze to a shape that suits your face, then tinted to match.

Lash extensions and lifts

Classic, hybrid, and volume sets, plus lifts and tints for a lower maintenance look.

Waxing

Legs, underarms, bikini, and facial waxing, with hot wax on the more delicate areas.

Massage

Back, neck, and shoulder work for desk bound shoulders, in 30 or 60 minutes.

Makeup for events

Bridal, occasion, and lesson appointments, with a trial where it matters.
