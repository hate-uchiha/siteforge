Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a patch test?

Tinting, lashes, and waxing need a patch test 24 to 48 hours before a first appointment. It takes two minutes and it is free.

How often should I come back?

Brows every three to four weeks, lashes every two to three, facials every four to six. If your skin wants longer between visits, we will say so.

What if my skin is sensitive?

Tell us when you book. We will pick gentler products and a shorter treatment, or say honestly that it is a job for a doctor or a dermatologist.

Can I book before an event?

Yes, and the timing matters. Brows and lashes want a few days, waxing wants 48 hours, and a facial wants at least a week.

Do you sell packages?

There are course rates for people who come regularly, but nothing is pushed at the desk. Pay as you go is fine.

Need it sorted this week?

Tell us what you need and we will send a written price, usually the same day.
