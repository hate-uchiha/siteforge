Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment?

Walk ins are usually fine on a weekday, but evenings and Saturdays go early. A message the day before gets you the time you want.

How long does a set last?

Gel and builder gel are normally good for two to three weeks before infills. Acrylics last longer if you come back for infills instead of picking at them.

Do you remove nails done somewhere else?

Yes. Removal and a tidy up is its own appointment, and the natural nail is checked before anything new goes on top of it.

How do you keep the tools clean?

Files and buffers are single use. Clippers, cuticle tools, and drill bits are sterilised between every client, and you are welcome to ask to see it.

Can you match a colour from a photo?

Usually. Bring the photo in and we will show you the closest colours we stock before starting, so there is no surprise at the end.

Need it sorted this week?

Tell us what you need and we will send a written price, usually the same day.
